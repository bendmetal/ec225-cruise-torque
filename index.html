Cruise Power Margin (FLI = 0.5 Below MCP)
Unchecked, cruise may plan right up to AEO Max Continuous. Checked: plan cruise 7% torque below AEO Max Continuous, IAW Flight Crew Operating Manual (FCOM). Costs a few knots where power is already near the limit — heavy, hot, or high.
